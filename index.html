Client form

Track which clients have returned your Google Form. Once it's connected, every client and booking shows Form ✓ or No form yet, with a Send form button.

One-time setup (about 10 minutes, on a computer)
  1. Tap Generate below to make a secret key.
  2. Tap Copy script. It copies the script with your key already in it.
  3. Open the form in Google Forms (the edit view). Click the ⋮ menu at the top right, then Apps Script.
  4. Delete what's in the editor, paste the script, and click Save (the disk icon).
  5. Click Deploy → New deployment. Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then click Deploy.
  6. Google asks for permission. Click Authorize access and pick your account. If it says "Google hasn't verified this app", click Advanced → Go to … (unsafe). That warning appears because the script is your own and not published, and that's normal.
  7. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec), paste it below, and tap Test connection.
